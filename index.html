05 Brand Guidelines
Tone, Voice & Usage
Brand Voice
Confident — Warm
Visionary — Precise
Celebratory — Refined
Speak as a trusted creative partner. Never boastful — always assured. Language is warm but precise, luxurious but never inaccessible.
Logo Rules
· Minimum clear space = height of "B"
· Never stretch or distort the mark
· Never place on busy backgrounds
· Use only approved color versions
· Minimum logo width: 80px digital / 20mm print
